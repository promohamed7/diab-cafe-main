## Brand & Style

The brand captures the warmth, golden intimacy, and quiet prestige of an artisanal specialty coffee roastery. It caters to coffee connoisseurs, boutique cafe patrons, and culinary enthusiasts seeking craftsmanship and heritage. The emotional response evoked is sun-drenched hospitality, sensory comfort, and refined craft—reminiscent of golden hour light streaming through tall cafe windows onto warm travertine surfaces, raw linen, and freshly roasted single-origin beans.

The design style combines **Luminous Minimal Luxury** with **Warm Amber Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Roastery Warmth**. Moving away from cold charcoal or harsh dark themes, the interface breathes through sunlit parchments, velvety crema tones, brushed copper-amber metallic highlights, and deep roasted espresso accents. Layouts prioritize generous breathing room, editorial typography, fine hairline dividing strokes, and frosted translucent overlays that simulate backlit cafe glass and amber brew vessels.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in natural coffee terroir, golden roasting profiles, and warm architectural stone.

- **Primary (`#2C1810`) — Espresso Noir:** Deep, full-bodied roasted bean pigment used for high-contrast headlines, primary button surfaces, vital icons, and high-impact structural anchors. Never pitch black; it retains warm reddish-brown undertones.
- **Secondary (`#C88A36`) — Roast Amber Gold:** A rich roasted honey and amber hue derived from caramelized sugars and copper roasting drums. Employed for active states, key interactive highlights, ratings, luxury tags, and warm glowing borders.
- **Tertiary (`#B85D38`) — Sun-baked Terracotta:** An earthy, aromatic clay tone for special accents, origin roast tags (such as Ethiopian or Arabian blends), badges, alerts, and subtle warmth shifts.
- **Neutral (`#FBF7F0`) — Artisan Parchment:** The core canvas backdrop. A luminous, soft limestone and warm cream that eradicates sterile hospital whites in favor of radiant tactile warmth.
- **Supporting Surface Tones:**
  - Cream Limestone Canvas: `#F5EFEB` for secondary cards, table headers, and container layers.
  - Warm Sand Fill: `#EFE6DA` for interactive hover fills, dividers, and disabled pills.
  - Warm Border Filament: `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08)` to `rgba(200, 138, 54, 0.22)` for quiet editorial definition.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial rhythm between high-prestige serif headlines and exceptionally crisp humanist sans body copy. 

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the heritage of European cafes and classical roasteries. Used with italic variations for origin notes, tasting descriptors (e.g., *Notes of Bergamot, Cardamom & Dark Cocoa*), and primary section titles.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Chosen for its humanist geometry, generous counters, and optical balance at tiny sizes on mobile viewports. For bilingual Arabic integration, pair with a clean, high-legibility geometric Naskh such as IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Cairo at matching optical weights.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize tight negative tracking for monumental impact, while small uppercase category tags (`label-sm`, `label-md`) use extended letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mirror luxury packaging stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is based on a responsive fluid grid with deliberate asymmetrical editorial breaks. It evokes the feeling of browsing an open-format luxury print menu or boutique lifestyle publication.

- **Grid Systems:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width 1320px, with `margin` of `2.5rem` and `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Elements span 3, 4, 6, or 8 columns.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and margins.
  - **Mobile (<640px):** 4-column layout with `gutter-mobile` (`0.75rem`) and `margin-mobile` (`1.25rem`), keeping content snug while preserving generous vertical negative space between sections.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** High breathing room around featured roastery goods prevents clutter. Cards maintain internal padding of `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), while compact list cells default to `space-md` (`1rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, sun-lit layered planes rather than cold grey drop-shadows.

1. **Backdrop & Parchment Planes:** The lowest plane is the warm canvas (`#FBF7F0`). Content cards float slightly above with `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF4EC`.
2. **Ambient Solar Shadows:** Shadows are tinted with warm espresso and roasted amber:
   - *Soft Rest:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.03)`
   - *Elevated Lift / Hover:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.09), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(200, 138, 54, 0.12)`
3. **Amber Glassmorphism:** Translucent overlays use `rgba(251, 247, 240, 0.78)` to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a backdrop blur of `16px` to `24px` and a hairline border of `1px solid rgba(200, 138, 54, 0.2)`. This creates floating frosted panes that reveal warm ambient cafe photography beneath.
4. **Low-Contrast Filament Borders:** Cards utilize whisper-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.07)`) to define structure without visual weight.

## Shapes

The interface embraces **Level 2 (Rounded)** curvature. Elements carry a gentle, tailored softness (0.5rem / 8px for standard components, 1rem / 16px for surface cards, and 1.5rem / 24px for feature banners).

This radius harmonizes with the soft geometry of roasted coffee beans, rounded ceramic espresso cups, and curved brass roastery fittings. Interactive pills (tags, filters, and chips) adopt full stadium pill styling for quick tactile scanning.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Filled with Espresso Noir (`#2C1810`), high-contrast parchment text (`#FBF7F0`), radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. On hover: subtle scale transform (`1.02`), soft gold outer aura (`0 0 16px rgba(200, 138, 54, 0.35)`).
  - *Secondary / Amber Accent:* Filled with warm amber gold (`#C88A36`), rich espresso typography, ideal for ordering or direct conversion.
  - *Outline / Ghost:* Parchment background with hairline border (`1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.15)`), shifting on hover to amber border and subtle crema tint (`#F5EFEB`).
- **Chips & Filter Pills:**
  - Fully rounded stadium borders (`9999px`).
  - Inactive: Background `#F5EFEB`, border `1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)`, label text `#3D2314`.
  - Active: Background `#2C1810`, text `#FBF7F0`, with optional warm amber pip indicator (`#C88A36`).
- **Cards (Product & Roast Origin):**
  - Surface: Warm artisan white (`#FFFFFF` on `#FBF7F0` ground).
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.07)` with optional warm amber glow on hover.
  - Structure: Upper image area framed with smooth inner corners, followed by generous padding (`1.5rem`), Playfair Display item title, tasting profile metadata in muted terracotta (`#B85D38`), and pricing in bold espresso.
- **Lists & Menu Rows (Traditional Cafe & Roastery):**
  - Alternating subtle rows or clean hairline dividing borders (`rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)`).
  - Bilingual alignment support (Arabic title on right / Latin translation on left, or synchronized top-bottom stack).
  - Price tags positioned in bold tabular numbers with warm amber accents.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Background: `#FAF4EC`, border `1px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.12)`.
  - Focus state: Border transitions to `#C88A36` with a soft ambient amber halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 138, 54, 0.15)`).
  - Placeholder text in soft espresso tint (`rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.4)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Custom rounded squares (radius `4px`) and circles.
  - Unchecked: `1.5px solid rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.25)` on `#FAF4EC`.
  - Checked: Solid `#2C1810` fill with a warm golden checkmark icon (`#C88A36`).
- **Signature Roast Badges:**
  - Micro-capsule pills with terracotta (`#B85D38`) or roast gold (`#C88A36`) tint, communicating roast degree (*Light City, French Roast, Arabic Cardamom Infusion*).
